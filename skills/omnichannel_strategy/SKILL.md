# Omnichannel Marketing Campaign Strategy Skill

## Strategy Framework Template

Every marketing strategy generated by the Strategy Agent MUST follow this structured format:

### 1. Executive Summary & Business Objective
- Primary goal (e.g. "Re-engage 15% of At-Risk Premium customers within 30 days").
- Key metrics: Revenue target, Target CAC, Expected ROI.

### 2. Customer Cohort Analysis & Segmentation
- Target Segment(s) derived from Analytics Agent findings.
- Primary pain points and motivation factors.

### 3. Channel Strategy & Timing Matrix
| Channel | Target Audience | Content Type | Dispatch Cadence | Expected CTR |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Email | At-Risk Premium | VIP Re-engagement Offer | Day 1, Day 7 | 4.5% |
| Push | At-Risk Premium | Limited-Time Discount | Day 3 | 2.1% |
| Retargeting Ads | Champions & At-Risk | Lookalike / Upsell Creatives | Continuous (14d) | 1.8% |

### 4. A/B Testing & Optimization Hypotheses
- **Hypothesis A**: Offering a 20% discount will drive higher conversion than free shipping for At-Risk Premium users.
- **Hypothesis B**: Personalized subject lines with first name boost open rate by >25%.

### 5. Success Metrics & Measurement Framework
- Conversion Rate (%)
- Total Incremental Revenue ($)
- Campaign ROI multiplier
