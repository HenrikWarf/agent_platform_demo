# Omnichannel Marketing Campaign Strategy Skill — Crazy Fashion

## Strategy Framework Template

Every marketing strategy generated for Crazy Fashion MUST follow this structured format:

### 1. Executive Summary & Business Objective
- Primary goal aligned with Crazy Fashion's brand values (e.g. "Re-engage 20% of Dormant At-Risk customers with our Autumn/Winter collection within 30 days").
- Key metrics: Revenue target (EUR), Target CAC, Expected ROI.

### 2. Customer Cohort Analysis & Segmentation
- Target Segment(s) derived from Analytics Agent findings.
- Segments: VIP Fashionistas, Loyal Regulars, Seasonal Shoppers, New Explorers, Dormant At-Risk.
- Crazy Club loyalty tier considerations (Platinum, Gold, Silver, Bronze).
- Primary pain points and motivation factors.

### 3. Channel Strategy & Timing Matrix
| Channel | Target Audience | Content Type | Dispatch Cadence | Expected CTR |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Email | VIP Fashionistas | Early Access to New Collection | Day 1, Day 5 | 5.2% |
| App Push | Loyal Regulars | Flash Sale Notification | Day 2 | 3.8% |
| Instagram | New Explorers | Style Inspiration Reels | Continuous (7d) | 2.5% |
| SMS | Dormant At-Risk | Win-Back Offer (20% off) | Day 1, Day 14 | 1.9% |
| In-Store Events | VIP Fashionistas | Collection Preview Evening | Day 7 | N/A |

### 4. Campaign Pillars
Campaigns should leverage Crazy Fashion's core strengths:
- **Sustainability**: Highlight recycled materials, garment collecting, circular fashion
- **Crazy Club Loyalty**: Member-exclusive pricing, early access, bonus points
- **Seasonal Drops**: Time campaigns around 12 annual collection drops + flash collections
- **Inclusivity**: Extended sizes, adaptive fashion, gender-fluid styling

### 5. A/B Testing & Optimization Hypotheses
- **Hypothesis A**: Offering Crazy Club bonus points (2x points) drives higher conversion than a straight 15% discount for Loyal Regulars.
- **Hypothesis B**: Personalized "Your Style Pick" subject lines boost email open rates by >25% for VIP Fashionistas.
- **Hypothesis C**: Instagram Reels with user-generated content outperform brand-produced content for New Explorers.

### 6. Success Metrics & Measurement Framework
- Conversion Rate (%)
- Average Order Value (€)
- Crazy Club enrollment lift
- Total Incremental Revenue (€)
- Campaign ROI multiplier
- Cart abandonment recovery rate
