# Omnichannel Marketing Campaign Strategy Skill

## Overview
This skill guides the **Strategy Pipeline** (`strategy_reasoner` ➔ `strategy_formatter`) in formulating omnichannel marketing frameworks, ROI projections, channel mix distributions, and A/B test hypotheses for:
- **ICA Sverige**: Swedish Grocery & Retail (SEK currency, Stammis loyalty, store formats, seasonal food moments)
- **Crazy Fashion**: Nordic Fashion & Apparel (EUR currency, Crazy Club, fashion drops, sustainability)

---

## 1. Strategy Framework Structure

Every strategy formulation MUST follow this structured architecture:

### 1. Executive Summary & Business Goal
- Clear financial and behavioral objective (e.g. *"Reactivate 25% of Inaktiva Stammisar with seasonal meal kits"* or *"Drive €120k revenue from VIP Fashionistas for Autumn Drop"*).
- Target currency: **SEK (kr)** for ICA Sverige, **EUR (€)** for Crazy Fashion.

### 2. Customer Cohort Segmentation
- **ICA Sverige Cohorts**: `Barnfamiljer Storhandlare`, `Ekologiskt Medvetna`, `Lojala Veckohandlare`, `Prisjägare & Studenter`, `Inaktiva Stammisar`.
- **Crazy Fashion Cohorts**: `VIP Fashionistas`, `Loyal Regulars`, `Seasonal Shoppers`, `New Explorers`, `Dormant At-Risk`.

### 3. Exactly 4-Channel Distribution & Cadence
- Balance digital (App Push, Email, Social) and physical/direct channels (In-Store Banners, SMS, Direct Mail) with explicit percentage allocations summing to 100%.

### 4. Exactly 3 Core Strategic Pillars
- **ICA Strengths**: Matglädje & recept, Lokala klipp & Stammispriser, Hållbarhet & svenskt ursprung (KRAV / Från Sverige).
- **Crazy Fashion Strengths**: Circular fashion & recycling vouchers, Member exclusive drops, Trend-forward styling.

### 5. Exactly 3 A/B Testing Hypotheses
- Specific hypothesis statements with measurable KPIs (Open rate lift, Conversion rate lift, Basket size increase).
