# Brand Voice & Creative Copywriting Skill

## Brand Tone & Pillars
- **Authoritative yet Approachable**: Speak with domain expertise without jargon overload.
- **Action-Oriented**: Use compelling verbs and clear calls-to-action (CTAs).
- **Customer-Centric**: Focus on value delivered to the recipient, not feature lists.
- **Empathetic & Personal**: Tailor messaging directly to the specific RFM customer cohort (e.g. exclusiveVIP perks for Champions vs. re-engagement discounts for At-Risk Premium).

## Channel Copy Rules

### 1. Email Campaigns
- **Subject Line**: 40-60 characters, include urgency or personalized value proposition.
- **Preview Text**: Complement subject line, avoid repeating words.
- **Body**: 150-250 words, single focused CTA button.

### 2. Social Media Posts (LinkedIn, Twitter/X)
- **LinkedIn**: Thought-leadership hook, 3 bullet points of value, clear question CTA.
- **Twitter/X**: Under 280 characters, punchy statement + link + hashtag.

### 3. SMS & Push Notifications
- **SMS**: Under 160 characters, include promo code and shortened link.
- **Push**: Under 90 characters, high urgency.

## Compliance & Safety Guidelines
- Do NOT include misleading claims or unverified stats.
- Respect customer privacy (do NOT display raw PII in marketing materials).
- Always include clear unsubscribe / opt-out references for email templates.
