# Brand Voice & Creative Copywriting Skill — Crazy Fashion

## Brand Tone & Pillars
- **Confident & Contemporary**: Speak with fashion authority without being pretentious or elitist. Crazy Fashion is for everyone.
- **Inclusive & Warm**: Every customer belongs. Use language that welcomes all ages, sizes, genders, and budgets.
- **Sustainability-Aware**: Be honest about our progress. Highlight recycled materials, circular fashion, and garment collecting naturally — not preachy.
- **Trend-Forward yet Attainable**: Aspirational styling that doesn't feel exclusive. Make customers feel like they're making smart, stylish choices.
- **Nordic Simplicity**: Clean, direct communication. No unnecessary jargon or overly complex sentences.

## Channel Copy Rules

### 1. Email Campaigns
- **Subject Line**: 40-60 characters, include urgency or personalized value (e.g. "Your early access starts now, [Name]").
- **Preview Text**: Complement subject line, never repeat it. Mention Crazy Club perks or sustainability angle.
- **Body**: 150-250 words, single focused CTA button. Reference specific products or collections by name (e.g. "NOVA Blazer", "FRÖST Puffer").
- **Crazy Club Members**: Always acknowledge loyalty tier. Platinum & Gold get exclusive language ("As a valued Platinum member...").

### 2. Social Media Posts
- **Instagram**: Visual-first. Short, punchy captions (max 2 sentences). Use relevant hashtags (#CrazyFashion #SustainableStyle #NordicFashion). Tag products.
- **LinkedIn**: Thought-leadership on sustainable fashion, supply chain innovation. Professional but warm tone.
- **TikTok/Reels**: Trend-driven hooks, styling tips, behind-the-scenes content. Casual, energetic language.

### 3. SMS & App Push Notifications
- **SMS**: Under 160 characters. Include promo code and shortened link (e.g. "CRAZY20 for 20% off → crazyfa.com/sale").
- **App Push**: Under 90 characters, high urgency. Reference Crazy Club points or flash drops.

### 4. In-Store Event Invitations
- Personal, exclusive tone. Reference the customer's loyalty tier and location.
- Include event details: date, store address, RSVP link.

## Currency & Pricing
- Always use EUR (€) for pricing.
- Format: €XX.XX (e.g. €49.99, not 49.99 EUR).
- For discounts, show original and sale price: "~~€89.99~~ **€62.99**".

## Compliance & Safety Guidelines
- Do NOT include misleading claims or unverified sustainability stats.
- Respect customer privacy — never display raw PII in marketing materials.
- Always include clear unsubscribe / opt-out references for email templates.
- Sustainability claims must be verifiable (e.g. "made with 65% recycled polyester" not "100% eco-friendly").
