# Brand Voice & Creative Copywriting Skill

## Overview
This skill guides the **Content Pipeline** (`content_reasoner` ➔ `content_formatter`) in crafting brand-aligned marketing copy tailored to the active enterprise client:
- **ICA Sverige**: Swedish Grocery & Retail (SEK currency, Swedish language, Stammis loyalty program)
- **Crazy Fashion**: Nordic Fashion & Apparel (EUR currency, English language, Crazy Club loyalty program)

---

## 1. ICA Sverige Brand Voice Guidelines (Swedish Grocery)

### Brand Tone & Persona
- **Varm & Folklig**: Tala med värme, omtanke och glimten i ögat. ICA är hela Sveriges matbutik.
- **Matglädje & Inspiration**: Väck aptiten! Lyft fram goda smaker, säsongens råvaror och enkla middagstips.
- **Vardagsenkel & Tillgänglig**: Gör vardagen enklare och godare för alla, från barnfamiljer till studenter och pensionärer.
- **Hållbarhet & Svenskt Ursprung**: Lyft stolt fram *Från Sverige 🇸🇪*, *KRAV 🌿*, svenskt lantbruk och klimatsmarta val.
- **Stammis & Lokalt Värde**: Betona ICA Stammis-förmåner, personliga priser och den lokala handlarens engagemang.

### Copywriting Formats & Rules (Swedish)
- **Email & Nyhetsbrev**: Korta, inspirerande ämnesrader (t.ex. *"Veckans Stammispriser & Middagstips under 25 minuter 🍽️"*). Lyft recept, veckoklipp och ladda-till-kortet-knappar.
- **Sociala Medier (Instagram / Facebook)**: Matfokus, snabba receptfilmer, tips mot matsvinn, taggar som `#ICAMaxi #Stammispris #Matglädje #SvenskMat`.
- **SMS & App-Push**: Max 160 tecken, personlig ton: *"Hej [Namn]! Nu finns dina personliga Stammispriser laddade i ICA-appen. Se veckans klipp → ica.se/stammis"*.
- **Valuta & Prisformat**: Alltid SEK (t.ex. `24:90 kr/st`, `89:00 kr/kg`, `Spara 15:-`).

## 2. Crazy Fashion Brand Voice Guidelines (Nordic Fashion)

### Brand Tone & Persona
- **Confident & Contemporary**: Speak with fashion authority without being pretentious or elitist.
- **Inclusive & Warm**: Welcoming language for all body shapes, genders, styles, and budgets.
- **Sustainability-Aware**: Emphasize circularity, 100% recycled materials, GOTS organic fabrics, and garment collection.
- **Nordic Simplicity**: Clean, direct communication without jargon.

### Copywriting Formats & Rules (English)
- **Email**: Focused subject lines (40-60 chars), personalized value, single hero CTA, Crazy Club tier perks.
- **Social Media (Instagram / TikTok)**: Visual-first, styling inspiration, editorial captions, tag `#CrazyFashion #NordicStyle`.
- **SMS & App-Push**: Under 160 chars, clear promo codes (`CRAZY20`), expiry urgency.
- **Currency & Pricing**: All pricing strictly in EUR (`€49.99`, `€112.49`).

## 3. Strict Channel Scoping Rule
Produce creative copy ONLY for the specific marketing channel(s) requested in the objective:
- **Email Only**: Generate ONLY the email template.
- **Social Only**: Generate ONLY the social media posts.
- **SMS Only**: Generate ONLY the SMS copy under 160 characters.
- **Full Campaign**: Generate all three deliverables (Email, Social, and SMS).
