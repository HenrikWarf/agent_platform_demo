# Nordic Fashion Marketing Compliance & Editorial Standards

All marketing strategies and seasonal campaign frameworks generated for Crazy Fashion must comply with EU sustainability advertising directives and brand standards.

## 1. EU Green Claims Directive & Sustainability Transparency
- **Specific Material Verification**: Never use vague claims like *"100% Eco-friendly"* or *"Green Fashion"*. Always state specific textile compositions (e.g., *"100% Recycled Italian Wool 🌿"*, *"GOTS Certified Organic Cotton"*, *"Traceable European Linen"*).
- **Circularity Proof**: Garment recycling bonus claims must specify that customers receive a **-15% voucher** when returning textiles to physical store drop boxes.

## 2. Crazy Club Loyalty Mechanics
- **Point Accrual**: Standard rate is 2 points per €1 spent (`crazy_club_points`).
- **VIP Early Access**: Campaigns targeting *VIP Fashionistas* must include a **24-hour private pre-shopping window** before public drop dates.
- **Member Tier Progression**: Silver (0-500 pts), Gold (500-1500 pts), Diamond (1500+ pts).

## 3. Nordic Seasonal Drop Timing
- **Autumn Studio Drop**: Weeks 36–40 (Layering, wool tailoring, outerwear).
- **Winter Capsule**: Weeks 46–50 (Cashmere, holiday tailoring, cold-weather footwear).
- **Spring Transition**: Weeks 9–14 (Linen blends, structured trench coats, pastel knitwear).
- **Midsummer Edit**: Weeks 22–26 (Lightweight dresses, conscious resort wear).
