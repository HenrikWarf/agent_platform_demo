# RFM Customer Segmentation Methodology — Crazy Fashion

## 1. Segmentation Heuristics & Cohort Profiles

| Cohort Name | Recency (R) | Frequency (F) | Total Monetary (M) | Key Behavioral Traits |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **VIP Fashionistas** | High (< 14 days) | High (6+ orders/yr) | €1,200 – €3,500 | Studio Collection buyers, cashmere, tailoring. |
| **Loyal Regulars** | High (< 20 days) | Moderate (4-7 orders) | €600 – €1,400 | Dependable essentials and seasonal wardrobe updates. |
| **Seasonal Shoppers** | Moderate (30-60 days)| Moderate (2-4 orders) | €300 – €800 | Peak volume during Autumn/Winter drops & Summer sales. |
| **New Explorers** | High (< 10 days) | Low (1 order) | €80 – €250 | First-time buyers responsive to introductory vouchers. |
| **Dormant At-Risk** | Low (> 90 days) | Low (1-2 orders) | €100 – €400 | Churn risk > 0.70; responsive to win-back VIP discounts. |
