# Swedish Retail Marketing Compliance & Advertising Standards

All marketing strategies and promotional campaigns generated for ICA Sverige must comply with Swedish consumer protection legislation and ICA brand standards.

## 1. Prisinformationslagen & Discount Transparency
- **30-Dagarsregeln (30-Day Lowest Price Rule)**: Whenever a discount is claimed (e.g., *"Spara 20:-"*, *"Veckans Stammispris"*), the previous lowest price charged during the 30 days prior to the promotion must be referenced in the background.
- **Jämförpris (Unit Comparison Price)**: Packaged groceries and bulk produce must always compute or account for unit prices (`Jmf-pris: XX:XX kr/kg` or `XX:XX kr/l`).
- **Kombinationserbjudanden (Multi-Buys)**: Phrases like *"Köp 2 för 45:-"* must clearly state the ordinary single unit price (*Ord. pris 26:90/st*).

## 2. Stammis Loyalty Terms & Card Loading
- **Stammis-Only Pricing**: Promotions marked `Stammispris` require an active ICA Stammis membership.
- **"Ladda till kortet"**: This specific call-to-action should be used exclusively for digital clip-to-card coupons activated within the ICA app or web portal.
- **Stammis Points Multipliers**: Double point promotions (*"Dubbla Stammis-poäng"*) apply exclusively to ICA's own brands (ICA, ICA Basic, I love eco) or selected partners (Apotek Hjärtat).

## 3. Environmental, Health & Origin Claims
- **Från Sverige / Kött från Sverige**: Only products verified with 100% Swedish meat, milk, eggs, and processing may use the Swedish origin flag.
- **KRAV / Ekologiskt**: May only be claimed if certified under KRAV or EU organic inspection numbers.
- **Nyckelhålet**: Symbol for healthier everyday alternatives (higher fiber, less sugar/salt/saturated fat).
