# Marketing Channel Technical Constraints — Crazy Fashion

## 1. VIP SMS Constraints
- **Max Length**: Max 160 characters.
- **Tone**: Exclusive club invitation with personalized member link and points incentive.

## 2. Editorial Lookbook Email
- **Subject**: Max 45 characters, atmospheric collection title.
- **Preview**: Highlighting recycled materials or VIP pre-access window.
- **Body**: 100–140 words, evocative architectural narrative, fabric origin spotlight, high-contrast CTA button (`Shop the Studio Edit`).

## 3. Social Media (Instagram / TikTok)
- **Caption**: Short editorial mood description, fabric specifications, and collection drop date.
- **Hashtags**: `#CrazyFashion #StudioCollection #NordicMinimalism #ConsciousTailoring #RecycledWool`.
