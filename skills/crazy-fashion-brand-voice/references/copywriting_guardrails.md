# Brand Voice & Editorial Guardrails — Crazy Fashion

## 1. Core Brand Voice: *Editorial, Architectural & Conscious*
- **Tone**: High-contrast, confident, Scandinavian minimalism, understated elegance.
- **Language**: English with international fashion editorial poise, or bilingual Nordic context.
- **Philosophy**: Emphasize tactile craftsmanship, silhouettes, sustainability, and longevity over fast-fashion hype.

## 2. Terminology Guidelines
- ❌ **Avoid**: *"Super discount"*, *"Massive blowout sale"*, *"Cheap clothes"*, *"Must-have crazy bargain"*.
- ✅ **Use**: *"Studio Collection Drop"*, *"Conscious Italian Wool"*, *"Member Exclusive Pricing"*, *"Capsule Edit"*, *"Garment Longevity"*.

## 3. Emoji Guidelines
- Restrained, chic aesthetic: 🌿, 🖤, ✨, 🧵, 🧥. Maximum 1–2 per asset.
