# Brand Voice & Creative Copywriting Skill — Crazy Fashion

## Brand Identity & Tone
- **Voice**: Nordic contemporary, editorial, effortless elegance, inclusive, circular & sustainable.
- **Language**: English (Nordic market tone).
- **Core Themes**: Studio collections, sustainable fabrics (recycled wool, organic cotton), garment recycling drive (earn double Crazy Club points), Scandinavian minimalism.
- **Loyalty Program**: Crazy Club (earn 10 points per €1; Platinum member perks).

## Channel-Selective Deliverables
Generate deliverables strictly for requested channels:
- **Email**: `subject_line` (concise, captivating), `preview_text`, `body_html_or_markdown` (editorial tone), `cta_button` (e.g. "Discover the Drop", "Claim Voucher").
- **Social Media**: 2 Instagram/TikTok posts with engaging captions and 4-6 hashtags (`#CrazyFashion`, `#NordicStyle`, `#CircularFashion`).
- **SMS**: Time-sensitive, punchy copy strictly under 160 characters with CTA.
