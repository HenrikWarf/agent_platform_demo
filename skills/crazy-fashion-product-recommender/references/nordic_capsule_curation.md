# Nordic Fashion Capsule Curation & Wardrobe Architecture

## 1. The 5-Piece Nordic Capsule Structure
Every recommendation set curated for Crazy Fashion must form a coordinated, wearable wardrobe capsule:

- **Piece 1 (Hero Statement)**: Structured outerwear or tailoring (e.g. *NOVA Oversized Wool Blazer*, *Double-Breasted Wool Trench*).
- **Piece 2 (Core Layer)**: Premium knitwear or shirting (e.g. *Chunky Cashmere Roll-Neck*, *100% Silk Oversized Shirt*).
- **Piece 3 (Foundation Bottom)**: Clean-lined trousers or skirt (e.g. *Tailored Wide-Leg Pleated Trousers*, *Recycled Cotton Straight Jeans*).
- **Piece 4 (Footwear Anchor)**: Nordic climate-ready footwear (e.g. *Leather Chelsea Boots*, *Chunky Loafers*).
- **Piece 5 (Accenting Accessory)**: Finishing piece (e.g. *Heavyweight Recycled Wool Fringe Scarf*, *Minimalist Leather Tote*).

## 2. Color Palette Harmony
- **Nordic Neutrals**: Oatmeal heather, chalk white, taupe, camel.
- **Architectural Dark**: Midnight navy, charcoal slate, espresso brown, matte black.
- **Seasonal Accents**: Sage green, burnt ochre, dusty terracotta.
