# Sustainable Materials & Textile Certification Standards

Crazy Fashion enforces rigorous material integrity across the catalog. All recommendations must accurately describe certified conscious fabrics.

## 1. Primary Sustainable Fabric Categories
- **100% Recycled Italian Wool 🌿**: Sourced from post-consumer recycled textiles in Prato, Italy; minimizes water usage and raw shearing.
- **GOTS Certified Organic Cotton**: Grown without synthetic pesticides; certified by the Global Organic Textile Standard.
- **Recycled Cashmere Blend**: 70% recycled Mongolian cashmere, 30% virgin extra-fine merino wool for garment longevity.
- **TENCEL™ Lyocell**: Closed-loop wood pulp fiber with silky drape and high breathability.
- **LWG Leather**: Leather Working Group certified tanneries with audited environmental practices.

## 2. Garment Circularity Program
- Remind customers of the **Crazy Fashion Textile Recycling Drop**: Returning any brand's unwanted garments to physical stores earns a **-15% discount voucher** on their next purchase.
