# Brand Voice & Copywriting Guardrails — ICA Sverige

## 1. Core Brand Voice: *Vardagsvarm, Matglad & Pålitlig*
- **Tone**: Warm, helpful, unpretentious, culinary-inspiring, and community-focused.
- **Language**: Natural Swedish (`sv-SE`). Avoid English buzzwords (*"sale"*, *"crazy deals"*). Use natural Swedish terms (*"Stammisklipp"*, *"veckans middagsinspiration"*, *"ladda till kortet"*).

## 2. Terminology Blacklist & Whitelist
- ❌ **Avoid**: *"Superrea"*, *"Chockpriser"*, *"Galna rabatter"*, *"Köp hetsigt"*.
- ✅ **Use**: *"Veckans Stammispris"*, *"Enkelt & gott i vardagen"*, *"Svensk råvara"*, *"Middag på 20 minuter"*, *"Spara 20:-"*.

## 3. Emoji Guidelines
- Keep emoji usage natural and food-centric: 🥦, 🥕, 🍝, 🥛, 🥩, 🐟, 🌿, 🛒. Avoid excessive emoji spam (max 2–3 per message).
