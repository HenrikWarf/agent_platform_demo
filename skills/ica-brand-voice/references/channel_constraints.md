# Marketing Channel Technical Constraints — ICA Sverige

## 1. SMS Constraints
- **Max Length**: Strictly 160 characters (GSM-7 compatible).
- **Structure**: Brand hook + Offer headline + Savings amount + CTA link.
- **Opt-out**: Always end with opt-out suffix (e.g. `Avreg: 71120`).

## 2. Email Newsletter Specs
- **Subject Line**: Max 50 characters, curiosity-driven with meal benefit.
- **Preview Text**: Max 80 characters, elaborating on savings or recipe.
- **Body**: 120–180 words, warm opening, recipe steps, item price box, bold green CTA button (`Ladda Stammispris till kortet`).

## 3. Social Media (Instagram / Facebook)
- **Caption**: 80–120 words with ingredients list and simple 3-step dinner method.
- **Hashtags**: `#ICAsverige #Stammispris #Middagsinspiration #Vardagsmat #SvenskaRåvaror`.
