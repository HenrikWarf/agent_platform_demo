## 1. Segmentation Heuristics & Cohort Profiles

| Cohort Name | Recency (R) | Frequency (F) | Total Monetary (M) | Key Behavioral Traits |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Barnfamiljer Storhandlare** | High (< 7 days) | High (12+ orders/yr) | 4,500 – 9,500 kr | Large weekly baskets, staple nutrition, high volume. |
| **Ekologiskt Medvetna** | High (< 10 days) | Moderate (8-14 orders) | 4,000 – 7,500 kr | KRAV priority, high veggie/fresh seafood share. |
| **Lojala Veckohandlare** | High (< 5 days) | Very High (18+ orders) | 5,000 – 11,000 kr | Habitual midweek + weekend grocery shopping. |
| **Inaktiva Stammisar** | Low (> 45 days) | Low (1-3 orders) | 500 – 2,000 kr | Churn risk > 0.65, responsive to 20% Stammis win-backs. |
| **Prismedvetna Studenter** | Moderate (10-20 days)| Moderate (4-8 orders) | 1,200 – 3,000 kr | Low income bracket, high ICA Basic & promotional affinity. |

## 2. Metric Reporting Standards
- Always report monetary totals in **SEK (kr)** with thousand separators (`4 520 kr`).
- Display cohort distribution using proportional visual Unicode bars (`████████░░`).
