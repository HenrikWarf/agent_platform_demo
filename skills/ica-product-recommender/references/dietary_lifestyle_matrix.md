# Dietary Archetypes & Lifestyle Merchandising Matrix

## 1. Barnfamiljer Storhandlare (Family Wholesale)
- **Basket Goal**: Nutritious, quick-to-cook weekday meals with high volume-to-price value.
- **Anchor Items**: Svensk Kycklingfilé 1kg, Ekologisk Pasta 500g, Ekologiska Bananer, Mellanmjölk 1.5L, Köttfärs 500g.
- **Price Target**: 180–250 kr total basket.

## 2. Ekologiskt Medvetna (Eco & Health Conscious)
- **Basket Goal**: KRAV-certified organic, plant-forward, seasonal Nordic produce.
- **Anchor Items**: Havremjölk 1L, KRAV-morötter 1kg, Krossade tomater Eko, Färsk Laxfilé MSC, Grekisk yoghurt Eko.
- **Price Target**: 220–290 kr total basket.

## 3. Lojala Veckohandlare (Loyal Weekly Shoppers)
- **Basket Goal**: Reliable staples, premium fresh proteins, weekend coffee & treats.
- **Anchor Items**: Färsk Svensk Laxfilé, Svensk Hushållsost, Gevalia Bryggkaffe, Färsk Broccoli, Surdegsbröd.
- **Price Target**: 200–270 kr total basket.

## 4. Inaktiva Stammisar (Win-back & Re-engagement)
- **Basket Goal**: Deeply discounted high-perceived-value hero items to trigger a store visit.
- **Anchor Items**: Handskalade Räkor i lake, Helgbiff, Exklusiv glass, Apotek Hjärtat multivitamin.
- **Price Target**: 160–220 kr total basket with 20% member savings badge.
